# Zero-Nausea & Accessibility Gaming Ergonomics

Guidelines for configuring games, simulations, and 3D camera viewports to eliminate vestibular-visual motion sickness.

## Invariant Directives

1. **FOV (Field of View):** Must be configured to **90° – 105°** (prevents tunnel vision and fishbowl distortions).
2. **Motion Blur:** **MANDATORY OFF (0% / Disabled)** for both Camera Motion Blur and Object Motion Blur.
3. **Camera Shake & Head Bobbing:** **MANDATORY OFF / 0%** (disable screen shake during sprinting, recoil, and explosions).
4. **Frame Pacing:** Lock a stable frame rate (*Frame Rate Cap*) matching monitor refresh rate to eliminate nausea-inducing micro-stuttering.
5. **Optical Distortions:** Disable *Chromatic Aberration*, *Vignette*, and *Film Grain*. Set *Depth of Field* to off or minimal.
6. **Center Anchor:** Enable *Persistent Center Dot* or static crosshair to give the vestibular system a fixed focal point.
7. **Visual Fidelity:** Maintain crisp texture resolution (Anisotropic 16x, DLSS/XeSS Quality). Never drop rendering resolution to muddy textures.
